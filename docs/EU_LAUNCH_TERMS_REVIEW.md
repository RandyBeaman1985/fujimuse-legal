# EU launch terms review — 2026-11-25

Draft review for Clever Fox AI labs LLC as FujiMuse publisher. Goal: consumer iOS distribution in the EU. No organization contract playbook was found; the basis is general consumer-contract standards and the official sources below. This is preparation for qualified legal review, not a legal opinion or a compliance certification. Changes remain unpublished in PR 6.

## Main findings and proposed treatment

| Priority | Clauses | Previous language / issue | Draft treatment | Remaining decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Must-have / red | 4, 11–12 | “No refunds for partial periods”; blanket warranties and camera-damage exclusions | Preserve statutory conformity, updates, withdrawal and remedies; expressly subordinate limitations to mandatory law | Counsel must assess national implementation and Apple merchant responsibilities; do not substitute store policy for statutory remedies |
| Must-have / red | 15–16 | “Any disputes will be resolved through binding arbitration”; blanket class waiver; Arizona choice without consumer reservation | Remove compulsory arbitration/class waiver; voluntary complaint contact; preserve applicable consumer home-court and mandatory-law protections | Counsel review of all offered territories; fallback cannot waive mandatory consumer rights |
| Should-have / yellow | 13–14, 17 | Indemnity for any use; discretionary termination; unilateral changes | No consumer contractual indemnity; proportionate suspension and explanation; mandatory retrieval/remedies preserved; changes subject to required notice/consent | Confirm operational ability to handle reviews, export requests and notices before launch; narrower business indemnity retained |
| Should-have / yellow | 5, 8–10, 18 | Content license, platform scope, external services and Apple license interaction | Earlier draft restricts content license to requested services and separates Apple Standard EULA | Audit actual production processing, vendor agreements and license precedence; retain explicit iOS scope |

Exact insertion-ready wording is in terms-of-service.html; the Git diff against 5414ffd is the redline. These amendments are an integrated proposal, not separate alternatives to be selectively relied on without checking interaction.

## Whole-contract disposition

- Acceptance/account: present. Verify presentation and assent in final app. The 13-year minimum is not proof of GDPR child-consent or national contractual-capacity compliance; counsel must reconcile age policy and onboarding territories.
- Payment/renewal: present; purchase sheet, cancellation, refund process and lawful price-change notice require real Apple acceptance tests.
- IP/content: present; narrower service license. Asset ownership and permission for bundled photos/LUTs/icon remain an independent owner gate.
- Privacy: linked; verify GDPR lawful bases, international transfers, processor terms, request handling and retention against the actual restored production backend. A blanket reference to third-party terms is insufficient proof.
- Confidentiality, insurance, force majeure and B2B payment-net terms: no standalone provisions. Not automatically required in a consumer app service agreement; counsel can decide whether any fit this service.
- Assignment: present; now preserves consumer rights and obligations.
- Termination/survival: present; actual content retrieval and deletion operations remain acceptance gates.
- Apple terms: present; no new custom EULA is proposed.

## Official sources checked

- Digital content/services: Directive (EU) 2019/770, especially Articles 8, 13–19 and 22: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/770/oj
- Consumer choice of law: Rome I, Article 6: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2008/593/oj/eng
- Consumer courts: Brussels I bis, Articles 17–19: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2012/1215/oj/eng
- Withdrawal rights require transaction-specific review; Consumer Rights Directive consolidated 2026-09-27: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX%3A02011L0083-20260927
- Apple Standard EULA: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/

## Release decision

Keep EU readiness open until legal review, DSA verification, final purchase disclosure, privacy/transfer operations, and live legal-link verification are complete. Do not claim that text changes alone establish EU compliance. Prioritize mandatory rights, court access and actual operational remedies. Qualified legal counsel should review the draft before legal reliance or publication.
