FujiMuse Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

Introduction

FujiMuse ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the FujiMuse mobile apps, visit the FujiMuse website at fujimuse.app, or join the beta waitlist.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Information: Your email address and password when you create an account. FujiMuse accounts use email address and password sign-in only; we do not use third-party sign-in.
  • Profile Information: Username, display name, profile picture, bio, website, and camera model, if you choose to add them
  • User Content: Photos you post, captions, an optional location you type on a post, comments, likes, follows, and recipes you share
  • Recipes and Collections: Recipes you save and collections you build. If you turn on cloud recipe backup and sync, these are stored on our servers so they can sync between your devices.
  • AI Chat Content: Messages you choose to send through the AI chat feature
  • Photos for AI Preview: A photo you choose when you request an AI-enhanced recipe preview
  • Reports: Content or users you report, and the reason you give
  • Terms Acceptance: A record of when you accepted the Terms of Service and which version
  • Purchase Records: Google Play purchase tokens (and, for the iOS app, Apple App Store transaction identifiers) used to verify paid feature access. We do not receive your payment card details.
  • Beta Waitlist: Email address, whether and when you confirmed it through the link we email you, consent date and version, language/locale, referring page, landing-page variant, and campaign attribution parameters when you join the beta waitlist on our website. The waitlist does not require a FujiMuse account.
  • Support Messages: Anything you send us by email

Information Collected Automatically

  • AI Usage Records: For each AI chat or AI preview request we record the time, the feature, the provider and model, and token or image counts so we can enforce daily limits. These records never contain your message text or photos.
  • Analytics Data: Firebase Analytics is off by default. If you turn it on in the app, it collects usage events such as screens viewed and features used, device and operating system type, and the camera model you select, linked to your FujiMuse account ID.
  • Crash Reports: Firebase Crashlytics is off by default. If you turn it on, it collects crash logs and device state. Crash data does not include your messages, photos, or recipe details.
  • Server Logs: Our hosting providers record IP address, request time, and similar technical data when the app or website contacts our servers, for security and troubleshooting.
  • Website Analytics: The FujiMuse website uses Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights to collect aggregated page-view, referral, device, general location, and performance measurements, plus clicks on sign-up buttons. Vercel Web Analytics does not use third-party cookies, does not associate events with an individual or IP address, and discards its temporary visitor-session identifier after 24 hours. We do not send waitlist email addresses to Vercel Analytics.
  • Waitlist Abuse Protection: To limit repeated sign-up attempts, the website stores a keyed one-way digest of your IP address for a short rate-limit window. We never store the raw IP address with the waitlist.

Processed Only on Your Device

  • Recipe Text Recognition: When you import a recipe from a screenshot, text recognition (Google ML Kit) runs on your device. The image is not uploaded.
  • On-Device Preview: The standard (LUT) recipe preview runs on your device and does not upload your photo.
  • Camera Connection: Sending recipes to your camera happens over a USB cable between your phone and camera. We do not upload your camera's serial number.

How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the FujiMuse apps
  • Create and secure your account
  • Back up and sync your recipes and collections when you turn that on
  • Verify purchases and apply your subscription limits
  • Provide AI chat responses and AI-enhanced previews
  • Show your public profile and posts to other users
  • Review reports and enforce our content rules
  • Send technical notices and answer support requests
  • Understand app usage and fix crashes, only if you opt in
  • Detect and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Send requested beta invitations and the store launch notice
  • Measure which website pages and campaigns lead to waitlist sign-ups

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

AI Features and Data Processing

AI Chat

  • AI chat is available on paid plans. Messages you send are routed through our server (a Supabase Edge Function) to a third-party AI provider, together with the camera and recipe context the app includes to make answers relevant.
  • FujiMuse currently uses Google Gemini. Our server can also route requests to Anthropic Claude, and we will update this policy if our primary provider changes.
  • You can only send text. Your photos are never sent to the AI chat service.
  • AI chat messages are not stored by FujiMuse, on our servers or on your device. A conversation stays in the app only until you close it.
  • How long the AI provider keeps a request, and whether it may be used for training, is governed by that provider's commercial API terms.
  • AI-generated suggestions are creative guidance, not guaranteed camera or vendor facts, and should be verified before use.

AI-Enhanced Recipe Preview

  • When you request an AI-enhanced preview (available on paid plans), the photo you choose is re-encoded without location or other photo metadata and sent through our server to Replicate, together with the recipe settings.
  • Replicate runs an image model to generate the preview and returns a link to the result. FujiMuse does not store the uploaded photo or the generated preview on its servers. Replicate keeps them only as long as its data retention policy allows.
  • If you then post a preview to FujiMuse, it becomes User Content and is stored like any other post.

Public Information

FujiMuse has social features. Your username, display name, profile picture, bio, website, camera model, posts (including photos, captions, and any location you typed), comments, likes, follows, and shared recipes can be seen by other users. Photos you post and your profile picture are stored at links that anyone with the link can open. Your email address is never shown to other users. Photos are re-encoded before upload, which removes GPS and other photo metadata.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • With Service Providers: Account, database, storage, and server hosting (Supabase); AI chat processing (Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude if used); AI image preview processing (Replicate); in-app analytics and crash reporting only after you opt in (Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics); payment processing (Google Play Billing, and the Apple App Store for the iOS app); website hosting, aggregated website analytics, and performance monitoring (Vercel); waitlist database hosting (Neon); and waitlist confirmation emails (Resend). These providers process data on our behalf.
  • With Other Users: The public information described above
  • For Legal Reasons: When required by law or to protect rights and safety
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to this policy

Data Retention

  • Account, Profile, and Content: Kept while your account is active, or until you delete the item
  • Cloud Recipe Backups and Collections: Kept while your account is active and backup is on, or until you delete them
  • AI Chat Messages: Not stored by FujiMuse
  • AI Usage Records: Purged 35 days after they are created
  • Analytics: Up to 14 months in Firebase, only if you opt in
  • Crash Reports: Up to 90 days in Crashlytics, only if you opt in
  • Purchase Records: Kept while needed for entitlement, tax, accounting, refund, and fraud-prevention purposes
  • Moderation Decisions: Kept after account deletion with your identity removed
  • Beta Waitlist: Kept until beta and launch communications are complete, or until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, whichever comes first. An unsubscribed address may be kept in suppression form so we can honor the opt-out.
  • Backups: Encrypted database backups held by our hosting provider expire on their normal rotation

Deleting Your Account

You can delete your account in the app under Settings, then Delete Account. If you cannot sign in, follow the steps at fujimuse.app/account-deletion. Deletion removes your sign-in account, profile, posts and uploaded photos, comments, likes, follows, saved recipes, cloud recipe backups, collections, reports you filed, your terms acceptance record, and the subscription status we store. The page lists the limited data that is kept and for how long. Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription; cancel it in Google Play or the App Store.

To exercise these rights, contact us at davey@cleverfoxailabs.com. We may need to verify that the request comes from you, and we respond within 30 days.

Data Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including:

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encryption at rest provided by our hosting providers
  • Token-based authentication
  • Row-level security rules that limit each user to their own private data
  • AI provider keys kept on our servers, never in the app

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
